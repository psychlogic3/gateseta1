    Please read the instructions carefully

    General instructions

    1. Total duration of the test is 180 minutes. The clock starts when you press I am ready to begin and runs on its own; it cannot be paused.
    2. The countdown timer at the top right shows the time left. When it reaches zero, the test is submitted automatically.
    3. The question palette on the right shows the status of each question:
      1 You have not visited the question yet. 2 You have not answered the question. 3 You have answered the question. 4 You have NOT answered the question but have marked it for review. 5 You have answered the question and marked it for review.
      A question that is answered and marked for review will be evaluated.

    Navigating and answering

    1. Click a question number in the palette to go to it directly. This does not save the answer to the current question.
    2. Click Save & Next to save your answer and move on. Mark for Review & Next saves the answer (if any) and flags the question. Clear Response removes the saved answer.
    3. MCQ (multiple choice): one option is correct. MSQ (multiple select): one or more options are correct. NAT (numerical answer): enter a number using the on-screen keypad only.
    4. A virtual scientific calculator is available from the calculator button at the top.
